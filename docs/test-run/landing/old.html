What happens each month

  1. By the 6th

    Last month is closed. Every bank, card and insurer payment is matched to a booking, and your report arrives with any questions.

  2. On your payday

    Payroll is run. Payslips go to your team, RTI goes to HMRC and contributions go to your pension provider.

  3. Every quarter

    Your VAT return is filed through Making Tax Digital. You approve the figure before we file it.

Questions

We’re not VAT registered. Is Tallyroom still for us?

Yes. We do the books and payroll, and tell you when your taxable sales get close to the £90,000 registration threshold.

We pay more than 15 people.

Then we’re not the right fit yet. Tallyroom runs payroll for up to 15.

Do you do our year-end accounts?

No. We keep the books ready for your accountant, who prepares the annual accounts and tax returns.

What do you need from us each month?

Read-only access to your bank and clinic diary, and answers to the questions in your report.
