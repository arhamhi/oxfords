What we do, and what’s left for you

Every month

We match every payment in your bank, card terminal and clinic software to an invoice or a bill, then send you the summary.

Left for you: answer anything we couldn’t match.

Every payday

We run payroll for up to 15 staff, send payslips, work out pension contributions and report to HMRC.

Left for you: tell us about starters, leavers and changed hours.

Every VAT quarter

We separate exempt treatment income from taxable sales like classes and kit, prepare the return and file it.

Left for you: approve the figures before we file.

Questions

Anything else, email hello@tallyroom.co.uk and a bookkeeper will answer.

Do we have to leave our accountant?

No. Keep them for your year-end accounts and tax return. We send them finished books to work from.

Do we have to change our clinic software?

No. We work from your bank feeds and the payment reports your clinic software already produces.

Isn’t physiotherapy exempt from VAT?

Treatment by a registered physiotherapist usually is. Classes, massage by staff who aren’t registered, and kit sold at reception often aren’t. We work out which of your income is which, and whether you need to register at all.

What if we grow past 15 staff?

Tell us before it happens. We’ll say plainly whether Tallyroom still fits, and help you move payroll if it doesn’t.
