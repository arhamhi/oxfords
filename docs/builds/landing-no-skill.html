Questions

Fair things to ask.

Will reminders embarrass me in front of a client?

You see every template before it goes live, and you choose the tone. Reminders are signed with your name and never go out once an invoice is paid.

Do my clients need an account?

No. They open a link, see the invoice, and pay.

What if a client says they paid by bank transfer?

Mark the invoice paid yourself and reminders stop immediately. Partial payments are supported too.

Can I move my existing invoices in?

Yes, import open invoices from a CSV so reminders cover them from day one.

What happens if I drop back to Free?

Nothing is deleted. Automatic reminders pause, and you keep every invoice and record.
