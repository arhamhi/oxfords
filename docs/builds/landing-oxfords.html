Questions

Who do the reminders come from?

From your own email address, signed with your name. Clients reply straight to you, not to us.

What if a client says they already paid?

Mark the invoice paid in one tap and every remaining reminder is cancelled. Nothing goes out after that.

How do clients pay me?

By card or bank transfer from the pay page. The money goes to your account, and we do not hold it or take a cut.

Can I leave?

Yes. Export every invoice and client as a spreadsheet at any time, on the free plan too.
