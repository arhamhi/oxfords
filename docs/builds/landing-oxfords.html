Reminders go out on your schedule, in your words.

Set it once for every client, or change it for the one who always pays late. Each message names the invoice, the amount and how to pay.

  1. 3 days beforeA friendly heads-upThe invoice is due soon, with the link to pay.
  2. On the due dateA short noteDue today. Nothing else to do if it is already paid.
  3. 7 days lateA plain follow-upMentions the date it was due and the amount.
  4. 14 days lateA firm last reminderAsks for a payment date and copies you in.
